Explicit limitation/finding
FixtureG contains a complete argmax-failure log and complete JSON receipt,
but A.token=1,C.token=2 while A_vs_C.tok_same=1. Classifier returns CAUGHT0.
The numerical receipt details are syntactically parsed but not correlated
with one another or with the named FAIL line. Thus this is an outer-schema
and ordered-check gate, not an independent numerical evidence verifier.
The parent explicitly identified nested semantics as not yet enforced.
Recommendation: document that boundary. If broader receipt integrity is
claimed later, require nested field presence/types and consistency, beginning
with tok_same versus tokens and the selected numerical failure. This review
does not certify contradictory nested data as trustworthy evidence.
